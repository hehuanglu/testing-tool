The inventories below apply only to assigned questions; a technique name does not assign every product endpoint or role. From goals/source/spec/results choose meaningful input/risk/role/state questions, existing operations, baselines/fixtures, and sufficient outcomes. Rule/request counts and invented risk scores do not establish sufficiency; no new proof campaign is required per choice.
